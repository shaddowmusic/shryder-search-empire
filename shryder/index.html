Anti-impersonation checklist

How to verify SHRYDER

  1. Copy the complete mint address from this page.
  2. Open a reputable Solana explorer independently.
  3. Paste the address and confirm the network is Solana mainnet.
  4. Confirm the name and ticker, then compare all 44 address characters.
  5. On a trading interface, confirm the exact same mint before requesting a quote or connecting a wallet.

Security warning

Ticker names are not unique

A token labeled “SHRYDER” can still be an impersonator. Never rely on a logo, ticker, screenshot, social post or shortened address alone. No official source will need your private key or recovery phrase.
