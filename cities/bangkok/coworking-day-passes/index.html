Before buying access

Ask five questions that change the day

  1. Is my date covered? Confirm weekends, public holidays and the precise day-pass entry and exit times.
  2. Can I make calls? Ask about booths, room reservations, time limits and extra charges; a hot desk is not a private room.
  3. What does the total include? Check taxes, printing, refreshments, guests and any deposit or cancellation terms.
  4. How do I enter? Confirm the building lobby, floor, registration process and ID requirement.
  5. Can the space handle my work? Ask about seating, outlets, accessibility and connection needs. No independent noise or Wi-Fi speed test is claimed here.

A café, a free public area and a paid coworking hot desk are different products. Do not assume their access, call rules or amenities are interchangeable.

Day-pass questions

What a short stay actually buys

Can I buy a coworking day pass in Bangkok?

The Hive Thonglor, Common Ground and True Digital Park currently publish day-access options. Confirm the chosen location, date, access window and inclusions directly with the operator.

Does a day pass include meeting rooms and round-the-clock access?

Do not assume it does. Day-pass rights differ from full memberships; confirm room or booth use, guest rules, charges and access hours before paying.
