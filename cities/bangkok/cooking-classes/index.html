Match the experience

Choose what you want to learn

01

Blue Elephant: start with the session

The Sathorn school’s discovery program prepares four or five recipes in half a day. Its official page separates a morning market visit from the afternoon format and publishes menus by weekday, with an ingredient-availability caveat.

Choose morning when the market is the priority. For a particular dish, check the current menu rather than assuming every session teaches the same recipes. The Sathorn address is 41 South Sathorn Road, with BTS Surasak identified by the operator; do not confuse it with the separate Sukhumvit branch.

Check the Sathorn class and menu

02

Pink Chili: market plus kitchen

Pink Chili advertises market visits in both morning and afternoon classes, English-language instruction, your own wok, curry-paste making and four dishes to cook and eat. Ingredients and equipment are included in the published program.

This is a useful format to investigate if you want an afternoon market component. The school states that vegetarian options are available for its dishes. Confirm the actual substitutions and your session’s menu directly; that statement is not a promise of an allergen-free kitchen.

Check Pink Chili’s program

03

Thai Cooking Academy: menu choice

Bangkok Thai Cooking Academy emphasizes choosing dishes, making curry paste and cooking hands-on in an air-conditioned classroom. Its standard class page includes a fresh-market visit and BTS On Nut pickup, while the FAQ notes that market arrangements depend on the selected program.

Read the course selector carefully: half-day cooking, vegetarian classes and longer training are different products. Confirm the dishes you will personally prepare, the meeting point and whether the tour is part of that exact booking.

Before booking

Five details that change the day

  1. Choose the session, not just the school. Confirm market inclusion, the date’s menu and the total time including shopping and eating.
  2. Separate cooking from watching. Ask which steps you do yourself, whether stations are shared and which items are prepared for you.
  3. Describe dietary requirements in advance. Ask about sauces, curry paste and shared equipment, not just the main protein. Request a direct answer from the school before paying.
  4. Use the booking confirmation for arrival. A school address, BTS pickup point and market meeting location can be different places. Confirm the branch and late-arrival procedure.
  5. Check the terms. Recheck price, inclusions, cancellation, age requirements, accessibility and clothing guidance for your chosen course.

Do not schedule another large meal immediately afterward without checking the class format: these programs include eating what you make. Leave room for the transfer before an evening reservation.

Class questions

Menu, market and meeting point

Does every Bangkok cooking class include a market visit?

No. Blue Elephant Sathorn describes its market visit as morning-only. Pink Chili advertises morning and afternoon visits. Check the exact course at the Academy because its FAQ qualifies tours by program.

Can I choose every dish?

Do not assume so. Blue Elephant publishes changing daily menus; the Academy emphasizes menu selection. Ask what is selectable and what is demonstrated or served before choosing a course.

Is a vegetarian option the same as an allergy guarantee?

No. Ask the operator to confirm ingredients, substitutions and shared-equipment arrangements for your requirements. This guide does not certify any kitchen or dietary accommodation.
