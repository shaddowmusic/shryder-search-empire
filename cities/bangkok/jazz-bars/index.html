What each room gives the music

01

The Bamboo Bar: heritage

Choose it when the room, ritual and history matter alongside the band. Mandarin Oriental identifies The Bamboo Bar as a live jazz-and-blues venue and publishes an evening dress policy. Its official page currently says the bar does not accept reservations.

Check The Bamboo Bar directly

02

The Living Room: listening room

Choose it when you want a purpose-built jazz evening in central Sukhumvit. The official Marriott page emphasizes live jazz and the caliber of musicians, making the performance—not a general party—the anchor.

Check The Living Room directly

03

Saxophone Pub: casual energy

Choose it when jazz and blues belong in a pub rather than a luxury-hotel setting. The operator traces the venue to 1987 and publishes its Victory Monument contact details, giving the night a grounded neighborhood anchor.

Check Saxophone Pub directly

04

ThreeSixty: river and skyline

Choose it when you want live jazz to meet a high Chao Phraya view. Hilton's official page pairs panoramic city scenery with recurring live sets; schedules are volatile, so confirm the exact performance before traveling.

Planning questions

Bangkok live jazz

Which Bangkok jazz bar should I choose?

Choose The Bamboo Bar for heritage and formal atmosphere, The Living Room for a dedicated hotel listening room, Saxophone Pub for casual jazz-and-blues energy, or ThreeSixty for live music with a river and skyline view.

Do I need a reservation?

Policies differ. The Bamboo Bar currently says it does not accept reservations, while other venues may use reservations or event-specific seating. Verify with the operator before going.

What should I check on the day?

Confirm the current performer, set times, reservation or walk-in rules, minimum age, dress policy and your return route.
