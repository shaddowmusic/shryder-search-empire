Planning questions

Bangkok above street level

Which Bangkok rooftop bar should I choose?

Choose Sky Bar for an internationally recognized icon, Octave for a Thonglor sunset and panoramic outlook, Sky Beach for a high-energy sky-high setting, or Spectrum when live-music programming is part of the plan.

Should I reserve?

Policies differ. Lebua currently lists Sky Bar as walk-in only, while other rooftops may use reservations, admission tickets or event-specific rules. Verify directly with the operator.

What should I check before going?

Confirm current hours, reservations or tickets, age and dress rules, weather policies and your return route on the day of the visit.
