Which experience fits your plan?

01

Mahanakhon: indoor plus glass-floor rooftop

The official SkyWalk page describes the 74th-floor indoor observatory and the outdoor 78th-floor area, including the Glass Tray and The Peak. Its published address is 114 Narathiwas Ratchanakarin Road, with Chong Nonsi BTS as the rail arrival option.

Choose this format if the glass-floor experience is the reason for going. Do not treat indoor access as a guarantee that the rooftop is open: the operator’s safety policy says rain or storms can temporarily close the outdoor deck, Glass Tray and The Peak.

02

Baiyoke: observation floor and revolving viewpoint

Baiyoke’s own observatory page distinguishes the deck on floor 77 from the revolving viewpoint on floor 84. The hotel is in Pratunam at 222 Soi Ratchaprarop 3, Ratchaprarop Road. Check which areas your selected admission covers instead of assuming every ticket has identical inclusions.

The hotel’s Bangkok Sky Restaurant page also advertises entry to both viewing floors with that dining offer. If you want a meal, compare the combined package before buying separate admission; reconfirm its current terms rather than assuming every restaurant booking includes the decks.

Before booking

Plan the view, then the evening

  1. Decide what is essential. An indoor panorama, glass floor, revolving viewpoint and dinner are different priorities. Choose the product that explicitly covers yours.
  2. Check the date’s access. Read the last-entry time and any outdoor-area notice. Opening hours for a restaurant or another attraction are not automatically the observatory’s ticket window.
  3. Leave weather flexibility. Check the forecast and the operator’s closure and rebooking terms. A purchased ticket does not guarantee clear visibility, a particular sky color or outdoor access.
  4. Allow for the process. Budget time for the journey, ticket checks, security and lifts before arranging a separate evening reservation. No fixed queue-time estimate is claimed here.
  5. Ask about your needs. Confirm step-free routes, lift transfers, seating, child rules and any mobility-equipment restrictions directly with the venue.

Continue after the view

A deck is not the whole night

Return to the Bangkok city hub, compare rooftop bars when drinks and atmosphere are the goal, or choose a live-jazz room for the next stop. Observation admission does not imply a bar table or music-venue entry.

Photography and gear

Visitor entry is not a production permit

Mahanakhon’s policy prohibits tripods and monopods and requires prior management approval for professional photographic or recording equipment. Check its current rules before bringing a camera setup. For Baiyoke, ask the operator about your proposed gear rather than assuming another tower’s rules apply.

For casual photos, plan around the actual light and follow staff instructions. Keep walkways clear and do not promise a shoot to a client before checking the venue’s permissions. This guide makes no claim of firsthand filming access.

Skyline questions

Views, tickets and weather

Which deck is better?

Choose by format: a glass-floor rooftop with an indoor viewing level, or an observation floor with a higher revolving viewpoint. Check the current ticket and your comfort with heights; this is not a firsthand quality ranking.

Should I book an exact sunset time?

Only after checking the date’s sunset, forecast and ticket window. Leave time for arrival and entry. No view or sunset is guaranteed, and outdoor areas may be unavailable.

Are meals and drinks included?

Only when the selected product says so. Baiyoke’s linked dining offer includes viewing-floor entry, but that does not establish the terms of every admission or restaurant package.
