What each market night actually gives you

01

Asiatique: riverfront and easy variety

Choose Asiatique when a broad, polished destination is more important than a traditional market feel. The official site combines shopping, dining, sightseeing, activities and events on the Chao Phraya. Treat individual attraction tickets and schedules as separate decisions.

02

ChangChui: creative and independent

Choose ChangChui when art, design and an unconventional setting matter more than central-city convenience. The operator publishes distinct green-zone and night-zone hours and identifies Wednesday as the closed day; verify any event program separately.

Check ChangChui directly

03

JODD FAIRS: food and MRT access

Choose the current Ratchada market when food variety and rail access lead the plan. A recent local guide, updated 5 October 2026, places it on Ratchadaphisek Road near Thailand Cultural Centre MRT. Because location history is confusing, confirm the current pin before leaving.

Check the current location guide

04

Yaowarat: the district is the route

Choose Yaowarat when you want to walk through a food-and-market district rather than browse one managed venue. Keep the route compact and expect individual vendors to set their own days and hours.

Planning questions

Bangkok market nights

Which Bangkok night market should I choose?

Choose Asiatique for riverside polish, ChangChui for creative-space atmosphere, JODD FAIRS Ratchada for a food-heavy modern market, or Yaowarat for a whole street-food district.

Where is JODD FAIRS now?

Current October 2026 sources place JODD FAIRS Ratchada on Ratchadaphisek Road near Thailand Cultural Centre MRT. Older Rama 9 pages remain online, so verify the live operator location before traveling.

What should I check on the day?

Confirm the current location, operating day, hours, weather notice and final transport connection. Individual vendor schedules can differ from the destination's general hours.
