Before paying

A record is more than its cover

  1. Identify the edition. Check artist, title, catalog number, format and pressing details. If an original pressing matters, ask the seller how it is identified.
  2. Separate disc and sleeve condition. Ask what the grading means and whether playback checks are available. Visual appearance alone is not a complete listening test.
  3. Check how you will play it. Confirm the record’s speed and your setup’s compatibility. For equipment, ask what else the system needs, including speakers or amplification.
  4. Know the shop’s terms. Clarify the final price, payment options, returns and whether a hold or online order can be collected in person.
  5. Protect the purchase. Ask for suitable packing and keep records away from direct heat. If shipping, agree on packaging, tracking and responsibility for damage before paying.

For a discovery visit, tell staff what you already enjoy—an artist, rhythm or instrument is a better starting point than asking only for something “rare.” No rarity or resale-value promise is made here.

From shopping to listening

Keep music at the center

Return to the Bangkok city hub, compare listening bars for a record-led evening, or choose live jazz bars when you want musicians on stage. Record shopping and a listening session are different visits.

Shopping questions

Make the trip useful

Where should I start for Thai records?

Zudrangma’s own catalog includes Thai molam and luk thung categories, alongside other Thai styles and international music. Use those categories to prepare a few listening references, then confirm current stock with the store.

Can I buy playback gear on the same outing?

Gadhouse’s FORMAT BKK Ari entry describes records alongside turntables and audio products. Confirm the current branch, product availability and any demonstration arrangements before visiting.

Why are opening hours not listed?

Live hours can change, and Zudrangma’s official contact page contains conflicting schedules. Direct confirmation is more useful than repeating one of those times as settled fact.
