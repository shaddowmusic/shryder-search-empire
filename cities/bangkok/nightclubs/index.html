Match the floor

Which Bangkok club format fits?

01

Sing Sing: theatrical electronic focus

Sing Sing describes a 1930s Chinoiserie theater spread across three floors and four split levels. Its published music identity is specific: house, melodic house, indie dance and afro house, rather than Top 40 or commercial EDM.

The venue publishes a 20+ rule, original-passport or Thai-ID requirement, and smart-casual restrictions including no open-toe shoes, sandals, singlets or sports shorts. Check the current night and use the venue’s direct booking route before going.

Check Sing Sing Theater

02

Levels: open-format range in one address

Levels describes a main room, dance-floor mezzanine and terrace. The published soundtrack crosses open format, commercial house and pop, while the terrace plays house and techno. That breadth makes it the most flexible option here when a group does not share one narrow genre.

The official page places the club in Sukhumvit Soi 11, recommends reservations and publishes smart-casual restrictions: no shorts, open-toe shoes, hoodies, vests, caps or sportswear. Confirm the current event and admission terms directly.

Check Levels Club

03

Mustache: house and techno with the lineup in front

Mustache’s official site centers house and techno, a VOID system and a 700-person room on Decho Road in Silom. Its event listings name the artists and subgenres, which makes the night’s sound easier to verify before buying a ticket.

The venue publishes Friday-and-Saturday core hours, but special and takeover events can use other places or windows. Read the exact event page. Its door rules state 20+, real ID, and no swim shorts, singlets or flip-flops.

Check Mustache Bangkok

04

Route66: pick a room inside RCA

Route66’s official event pages distinguish three zones: hip-hop in East, live band and K-pop in West, and EDM in South. That multi-room structure serves a different intent from committing to one specialist electronic floor.

The official site places the club at RCA and provides direct reservation contacts. Its archive also shows how event prices, table conditions and packages change. Use only the current event listing; an older party’s admission details are not a standing policy.

Check Route66 Club

Before the door

Build the night around one verified room

  1. Choose the sound first. Decide whether you want specialist electronic music, open format or a multi-room mix.
  2. Check the exact date. A club’s durable identity does not guarantee tonight’s lineup, room or ticket condition.
  3. Read the admission rule. Carry the identification the venue requests and confirm minimum age, dress code and entry terms.
  4. Separate ticket from table. A general ticket, guest list, reservation and table package are different products. Confirm what is included.
  5. Lock the return. Save the exact venue address and plan the final ride before entering; do not assume public transport will match the closing time.

Door questions

Music, ID and dress

Which Bangkok nightclub should I choose?

Choose Sing Sing for a theatrical electronic setting, Levels for open-format range, Mustache for a house-and-techno-focused room, or Route66 for separate hip-hop, live-band and EDM zones. Verify the exact night before traveling.

Do I need identification?

Rules differ. Sing Sing and Mustache explicitly publish 20+ entry and original or real-ID requirements. Check the selected venue’s current rule rather than assuming one club’s policy applies to another.

What should I wear?

Sing Sing and Levels publish smart-casual restrictions, while Mustache lists specific prohibited items. Read the official venue or event page on the day; this guide does not guarantee admission.
