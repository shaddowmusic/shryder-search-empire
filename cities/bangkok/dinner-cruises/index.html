What each cruise changes about the night

01

Manohra: heritage and a set menu

Choose Manohra when the vessel itself matters. The operator presents carefully restored teak rice barges and Thai cuisine rather than a large floating-party format. Its current dinner page publishes multiple evening rounds, but schedules and packages should be rechecked for your date.

Explore Manohra's official dinner cruise

02

Horizon: hotel-led buffet service

Choose Horizon when you want a cruise operated within Shangri-La Bangkok's dining program. Its official page describes an international buffet and a route past major historic river landmarks, with boarding at the hotel's NEXT2 pier. Confirm dress code and operating details before leaving.

Explore Horizon directly

03

Meridian: sunset or after dark

Choose Meridian when departure light and live music lead the decision. The operator currently separates a golden-hour sailing from its later dinner cruise; both use an international buffet, while the dinner program adds traditional Thai performances. Verify the current ICONSIAM check-in instructions.

Compare Meridian's official sailings

04

Chao Phraya Princess: sax to dance floor

Choose Chao Phraya Princess when live entertainment is central. Its official route program describes saxophonists and singers playing soft pop and jazz during the first part of the cruise, then a more upbeat hour for dancing. The operator publishes multiple program and pier details, so match your confirmation exactly.

Planning questions

Chao Phraya dinner cruises

Which Bangkok dinner cruise should I choose?

Choose Manohra for restored-rice-barge heritage and Thai set-menu dining, Horizon for a hotel-run international buffet, Meridian for sunset or later live-music programs, or Chao Phraya Princess for saxophone, singers and a more social finish.

Where do the cruises depart?

Departure points differ. Current official pages identify Anantara Riverside for Manohra, NEXT2 pier for Horizon, ICONSIAM for Meridian, and program-specific piers for Chao Phraya Princess. Verify the exact check-in and boarding point on your confirmation.

What should I verify before booking?

Confirm the sailing name, date, check-in desk, pier, boarding window, seating area, dining format, dietary-request process, accessibility, weather policy, cancellation terms and transport back from the pier.
