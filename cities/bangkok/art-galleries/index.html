Bangkok art galleries, one exhibition route at a time

Choose the work you want to see, then build the route around it. Bangkok’s art spaces range from a public cultural center to a dedicated exhibition venue and a riverside gallery hub. They are not interchangeable—and a building’s opening time does not guarantee every show is open.

Three anchors, two practical areas

These are useful starting points, not a complete survey or a ranking. BACC and Jim Thompson Art Center both reference National Stadium BTS in their visitor information. River City is a separate riverside outing. Start with one area instead of treating all three as a single uninterrupted walk.

What each anchor gives you

01

BACC: a public-art starting point

BACC’s visitor page places its main galleries on floors 7–9 and connects the third-floor entrance to National Stadium BTS. General exhibition entry is free except for special events. Check the program before deciding how long to stay; not every level serves the same purpose.

For the main galleries, photography restrictions include no flash or tripods, and video needs permission. Follow the signs for individual works and ask staff before planning a shoot.

Check BACC visitor information

02

Jim Thompson: choose the art-center visit

The Art Center publishes its own exhibition program and visitor information at No. 6 Soi Kasemsan 2. Its website distinguishes art-center-only admission from a combined House Museum and Art Center option. Decide whether you want the contemporary exhibition, the house visit, or both before selecting admission.

The operator identifies National Stadium BTS as an arrival option and requests advance reservations for groups. Use the current exhibition page rather than an old social post to choose your date.

Check the Art Center’s program

03

River City: a riverside gallery cluster

River City combines art galleries, antiques and an exhibition program at 23 Charoen Krung Soi 24 in Talat Noi. Its site separates gallery listings from what is currently showing. Use both: one helps locate a space, while the other identifies a dated exhibition.

Allow a separate transfer from the National Stadium area. The official visit page includes transport options, but reconfirm the route on your date rather than relying on an old fare or boat timetable.

Build the route

Look at the exhibition before the map

  1. Find one show that interests you. Check its opening and closing dates, venue and floor. A past listing can remain online after a show ends.
  2. Choose the area. Start with the National Stadium pair or a Talat Noi riverside visit. Add another district only after allowing transfer time.
  3. Confirm entry. Building access, exhibition admission, talks and workshops may have different terms. Check last entry, closed days and any reservation requirement.
  4. Check the way in. Ask about step-free access, lifts, lockers and the entrance that suits your arrival route.
  5. Keep viewing and filming separate. Ask about video, equipment and any commercial use before bringing a production setup. Photographing a room is not automatically permission to photograph every work or visitor.

This guide deliberately does not turn a temporary exhibition into a permanent promise. The linked programs tell you what is available for the day you actually plan to visit.

Let the evening follow

Continue through the city

Return to the Bangkok city hub, choose a listening bar for a record-led evening, or compare dinner cruises for a river night. A gallery visit does not include either next stop.

Gallery questions

Buildings, shows and tickets

Are these all individual commercial galleries?

No. BACC is a public cultural center, Jim Thompson Art Center is an exhibition venue, and River City is a larger arts-and-antiques center containing galleries. This guide compares visitor formats rather than treating them as identical businesses.

Can I combine all three in one day?

You can plan that, but the central-city pair and River City are separate route areas. Check the specific shows and entry windows before adding transfers; one well-chosen exhibition can be a more useful anchor than a rushed checklist.

Can I film a music or fashion video inside?

Do not assume visitor admission permits a production shoot. Contact the venue and follow its equipment, artwork and filming rules before making plans.
