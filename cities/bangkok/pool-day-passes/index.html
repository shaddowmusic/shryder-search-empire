What changes between the offers

01

VIE: pool time with credits

The official Edge Pool Bar page lists a pool day pass with food-and-beverage credit, a separate spa credit, showers and towels. The hotel is beside Ratchathewi BTS. Check which outlets and treatments qualify and whether credits must be used on the same visit. A spa credit should not be read as a fully included treatment.

NH: package names matter

NH’s official offer separates pool-and-gym, meal or drink combinations, and a pool-and-room package with limited room use. Non-hotel guests must collect a day-pass coupon at reception. The page warns that no lifeguard is on duty. Confirm the exact package, room window if relevant, and supervision arrangements before visiting.

Atrium: pool and fitness

Grand Mercure Bangkok Atrium explicitly lists outdoor swimming-pool and fitness-center access in its Day Pass Experience. Its address is 1880 New Phetchaburi Road. The text does not establish a room, dining credit or spa treatment as included; ask the hotel for the current full terms instead of assuming overnight-guest privileges apply.

Before booking

Six questions that protect the day

  1. Is my date available? Confirm reservations, capacity limits, blackout dates and the latest arrival time.
  2. What does this named package include? Separate pool, gym, towels, showers, lockers, room use and any treatment; do not infer benefits from a hotel facilities list.
  3. How do the credits work? Ask about eligible outlets, minimum spend, taxes, unused balances and same-day restrictions. A credit is not the same thing as unrestricted cash.
  4. Who can enter? Check child ages, adult supervision, visitor limits, swimwear and accessibility requirements directly.
  5. What happens if plans change? Ask about cancellation, weather disruption, pool maintenance and any change of date.
  6. How do I check in? Confirm the reception or pool desk, booking confirmation, any ID requirement and the final price including mandatory charges.

Pool admission is not an overnight reservation. If you need a private room, book a package that explicitly includes one and confirm its separate usage window. For swimming, check the venue’s safety rules and supervision rather than assuming a lifeguard is present.

Day-pass questions

Access is in the terms

Can non-guests use hotel pools in Bangkok?

These three hotels currently publish day-access offers. That does not mean every hotel pool is open to walk-ins. Confirm your date and obtain the required pass before using the facilities.

Does a pool day pass include a hotel room?

Not automatically. NH lists a distinct pool-and-room option; its ordinary pool packages are different. Always confirm room inclusion and the permitted room-use period.

Why are prices and fixed hours not copied here?

Offers and access windows can change, and packages are not directly interchangeable. The linked operator pages are the source for the current total and terms when booking.
