Two locations, different kinds of evening

Ibiza Town / Eivissa · Café or theatre

Café Pereyra and Teatro Pereyra

At Carrer del Comte de Rosselló, 3, the café and theatre share a building but offer different plans. The café describes live music, DJ sessions and jams; the theatre calendar includes concerts alongside comedy, parties and other formats. Read the individual listing if hearing live musicians is your priority.

Choose the right booking: A café table is not automatically a theatre ticket. Ask which room the music is in, whether a seat is included and what the entry rules are for that event. Pereyra’s FAQ distinguishes its winter cultural programme from its May–October dinner shows, so don’t book a winter trip around an old summer-show description.

Sant Carles · A dated electronic night

Akasha at Las Dalias

Akasha’s official calendar lists electronic-music events, including Supernova and Nido, beyond the summer season. Use the date-specific entry as your starting point rather than treating a residency name as a promise that the club opens every night or throughout every winter month.

Separate the spaces: Las Dalias lists market, café, garden and Akasha activities. A market visit or a garden session does not by itself establish indoor-club access. Check which areas your event uses, when each part starts, and whether a separate ticket or eligibility condition applies.

Make it the destination: Las Dalias is on the San Carlos road, outside Ibiza Town. Work out the return from the actual venue before choosing a late finish rather than adding this as an unplanned second stop.

Confirm the night before building the trip around it

  1. Match the date and format. Open the operator’s listing for your travel date. Check whether it is live music, a DJ set, a seated show or a daytime gathering; a venue’s artist archive is not its current programme.
  2. Confirm the exact admission. Save the room, start time, ticket or table terms, age and ID guidance, and cancellation rules. If seating or step-free access matters, get confirmation for the space you will actually use.
  3. Choose one anchor and a realistic return. Keep dinner near an Ibiza Town show, or plan a dedicated outward and return journey for Sant Carles. Check transport for your winter date and finishing time instead of relying on a summer timetable.

Year-round does not mean every night

A venue can offer year-round services while a specific show, room or residency has limited dates. If your travel date has no confirmed listing, keep the plan flexible rather than assuming an old poster will repeat.
