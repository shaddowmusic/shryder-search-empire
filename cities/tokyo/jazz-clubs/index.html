Blue Note Tokyo: the full evening

Blue Note Tokyo’s official reservation guide presents a performance-led dining room with seating areas, food and drink service, and date-specific booking. It suits a night where the room, table and stage all matter.

  • Select the exact performance before choosing a seat area.
  • Reserve through the venue’s official system; walk-in access is only possible when space remains.
  • There is no venue dress code, but the published performance rules still apply.

Cotton Club: reserved Marunouchi ritual

Cotton Club describes an all-reserved live restaurant and publishes table, box and pair-seat formats. Choose it when a planned seat and the Marunouchi setting are part of the night, not an afterthought.

  • Compare the official seat types before booking.
  • Use the venue calendar for the exact artist and date.
  • No dress code is published, but unauthorized recording during the performance is prohibited.

Shinjuku Pit Inn: program first

Shinjuku Pit Inn’s official English site separates morning, daytime and evening programs. GO TOKYO identifies the venue as an established jazz room dating to 1965. This is the live-house choice when the specific bill matters more than a dining format.

  • Read the official schedule for the applicable program.
  • Check whether reservations are accepted for that show.
  • Follow the published entrance order; an advance reference number can set priority.

Build the night around one set

  1. Choose the room format. Dining stage, reserved live restaurant and jazz-first live house are different evenings.
  2. Open the exact calendar entry. The venue name does not prove tonight’s performer, availability or admission conditions.
  3. Book through the official route. Keep the confirmation and understand the venue’s seating or entrance-order system.
  4. Map the return before the performance. Minami-Aoyama, Marunouchi and Shinjuku are different transport plans.
  5. Listen without taking the room. Silence devices, minimize movement and never assume photography or recording is permitted.

Room etiquette

The stage is the center

Blue Note Tokyo and Cotton Club explicitly prohibit unauthorized photography and audio or video recording during performances. Treat that as the baseline, then follow the exact announcement and staff guidance in any room.

Common questions

Booking, choice and room rules

Which Tokyo jazz club should I choose?

Choose by format: Blue Note Tokyo for a dining-led show, Cotton Club for an all-reserved Marunouchi live-restaurant night, or Shinjuku Pit Inn for a jazz-first live-house program. Check the exact artist before deciding.

Do Tokyo jazz clubs require reservations?

Policies vary. Blue Note Tokyo and Cotton Club publish online and telephone reservation systems. Shinjuku Pit Inn accepts reservations for applicable shows and uses advance numbers for entrance priority. Confirm the exact date on the official site.

Can I photograph or record the performance?

Do not assume so. Blue Note Tokyo and Cotton Club explicitly prohibit unauthorized photography and audio or video recording during performances. Follow the room announcement and staff instructions.
