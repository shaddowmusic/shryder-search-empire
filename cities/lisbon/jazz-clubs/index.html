Three ways to find a set

A dedicated club night

Távola Jazz Club

Start here when you want to choose a band and reserve a place at a club that programmes live jazz. Look up the artist in the venue’s concert agenda before using its separate reservations link. The club lists Rua Coronel Bento Roma 16.

A programme that moves between venues

Hot Clube de Portugal

Choose the show first. Hot Clube’s programme lists concerts at places including Teatro Variedades in Parque Mayer and MACAM on Rua da Junqueira, as well as dates outside Lisbon. Use the location and booking details on the individual listing; don’t navigate to a historic club address by default.

A bar night that can include jazz

O Bom, o Mau e o Vilão

The bar on Rua do Alecrim mixes jazz with soul, funk and electronic music, and lists both live performances and DJ sets. Check the artist and format for your date. This is the option when you’d enjoy the bar either way, not when a live jazz set is non-negotiable.

Check the bar’s programme

Check the lineup before leaving

  1. Want a concert? Look for a named ensemble and a start time. Confirm whether a booking guarantees a seat, whether there are multiple sets and how late arrivals are handled.
  2. Want to sit in? Look for a dated listing explicitly marked jam session. Ask the organiser whether visiting players can join, how sign-up works and what equipment is provided. A concert ticket does not grant stage access.
  3. Planning two stops? Map the confirmed performance address first and check the return from there. Save the event listing rather than relying on a venue name in a map app.

Booking labels to look for

Bilhetes means tickets; reservas means reservations. If both appear, check whether they refer to the same event and whether one covers a table rather than admission.
