What to check before you book

  1. Compare what the reservation covers. A concert ticket and a dinner menu are different purchases. Check drinks, optional extras, dietary needs and cancellation terms with the venue.
  2. Ask about timing and access. Confirm the arrival window, late-entry policy, stairs or step-free seating, and how long the evening usually runs. For a dinner show, ask whether there are breaks between sets.
  3. Listen when the room goes quiet. Save conversation for breaks, silence your phone and ask before filming.

Want the history first?

Visit the Museu do Fado for exhibitions. Book your evening performance separately; museum admission isn’t a show ticket.
