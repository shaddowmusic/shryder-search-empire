Make the visit worth the trip

  1. Check the edition. Bring the artist, title, label and catalogue number if a particular pressing matters. An online listing is not a guarantee that a copy is on the shop floor.
  2. Separate media and sleeve condition. Ask how each is graded and whether you may inspect or audition the copy. Do not assume listening stations or sealed-copy returns are available.
  3. Confirm the day’s opening and pickup terms. Ask whether a record can be held before making a dedicated journey. If you want to sell a collection, contact the shop first rather than carrying it in unannounced.
  4. Plan how to carry purchases. Protect sleeves from rain and keep records upright in a suitable bag. Decide whether you will take them with you or ask the shop about shipping.

Use Flur’s current address

Flur’s official contact page places it in Mercado de Arroios. Older record-shop guides may show its former waterfront address. Check the operator’s page before mapping your visit.
