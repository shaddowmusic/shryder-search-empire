Pick the base that fits the trip

South Beach · Suites and pool time

The Setai, Miami Beach

Start here for a stay built around the water. The hotel describes three temperature-controlled pools, with different pools for laps, lounging and families. Its room inventory distinguishes Art Deco Suites in the historic building from Ocean Suites with Atlantic views.

Before booking: Decide whether the room itself needs an ocean view. Don’t use the hotel’s beachfront setting as a substitute for checking your suite category. If a daybed matters, ask how access works; the hotel lists them as first come, first served.

Miami Beach · A hotel-and-show evening

Faena Miami Beach

Consider Faena if a theater performance is part of the reason for the trip. Faena Theater publishes its own shows and jazz series. The accommodation list separately labels Bay View, Partial Ocean View and Oceanfront rooms, so there is a meaningful choice beyond “a room at Faena.”

Before booking: Find the performance you want, confirm tickets, then match your room dates. Don’t assume a hotel reservation includes the theater or a restaurant table. Check the view label on the exact room rather than relying on a general ocean photograph.

Coral Gables · Mainland pool and golf

The Biltmore Hotel

Choose the Biltmore when the resort grounds matter more than a beach address. The Coral Gables hotel describes its pool, private cabanas and daybeds alongside an 18-hole golf course. This is a mainland stay, not an oceanfront alternative on Miami Beach.

Before booking: Ask which pool amenities are included, which need a separate booking, and how to reserve golf. If your main evening is at the Miami Beach Bandshell, plan that cross-city journey before deciding this is your base.

Explore the Biltmore’s rooms and facilities

Compare the actual stay, not just the nightly rate

  1. Match room category to the view. Save the exact room name, bed arrangement and view description. Ask about a balcony if one is important; “ocean view” alone doesn’t promise outdoor space.
  2. Separate room access from extras. Check cabanas, daybeds, theater tickets, golf and spa bookings individually. A facility being on the property doesn’t mean every service is bundled into the room.
  3. Read the total and the cancellation terms together. Compare the same dates and guest count, including any taxes, property fees, parking or deposits shown at checkout. Save the cancellation deadline in the hotel’s local time.
  4. Test the evening journey. Put the actual show address into your plan. South Beach, Faena’s Collins Avenue location and Coral Gables shouldn’t be treated as interchangeable starting points.

If the room view is non-negotiable

Ask the hotel to confirm the booked view category in writing before committing to a restrictive rate. A request for a higher floor isn’t the same as a guaranteed room type.
