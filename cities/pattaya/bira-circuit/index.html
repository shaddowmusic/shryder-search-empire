Bira Circuit Pattaya: verify the event before you travel

Bira Circuit is Pattaya’s strongest motorsport anchor—but a track booking is not automatically a public event. Use the official calendar to discover a date, then confirm the organizer, spectator access and arrival details before leaving the city.

Know the venue

A working circuit—not a walk-in tourist attraction

Bira Circuit opened in 1986 and is named for Prince Bira, Thailand’s first Formula One driver. Its official site describes a venue used for racing, vehicle and tire testing, product launches, exhibitions, promotional events and private team activities.

That variety is exactly why visitors must distinguish a public race from a private booking, test session, track day or corporate event. The calendar tells you the venue is occupied; the organizer tells you whether and how the public can attend.

The official address is 111 Moo 5, Pong, Banglamung, Chonburi 20150. This is outside Pattaya’s walkable beachfront core, so treat the circuit as a planned road trip rather than an extension of a city walking route.

The one rule

“On the calendar” does not mean “open to spectators”

Do not travel until the event organizer or circuit confirms admission, gate time, tickets and the correct entrance.

Choose your purpose

Four visits that require different confirmation

Spectator

Watch a race or public event

Confirm that spectators are admitted and identify the official ticket or registration source.

  • Ask: public or invitation-only?
  • Confirm: ticket, gate, parking and viewing area
  • Check: support races and final timetable
Participant

Join a car or motorcycle session

Use the session organizer’s entry rules rather than assuming the circuit’s general calendar is a registration page.

  • Ask: vehicle and driver eligibility
  • Confirm: helmet, license, inspection and briefing
  • Check: insurance, noise rules, fees and cancellation terms
Karting

Visit the kart facility

The official calendar separates circuit and kart activity. Contact the venue for the current public session, age, height, equipment and booking rules.

  • Ask: arrive-and-drive availability
  • Confirm: participant requirements and equipment
  • Check: whether a private event affects access
Media or organizer

Film, photograph or book the circuit

Commercial filming, paddock access and private track use require direct permission or booking.

  • Ask: accreditation or location agreement
  • Confirm: permitted zones, crew and equipment
  • Check: track hire, safety support and usage rights

The official-source route

Verify an event in four steps

Start with Bira’s Track Calendar

Use the circuit’s own calendar to identify the date and whether the listing sits under Circuit or Kart.

Identify the event owner

Find the championship, promoter, club, manufacturer or session organizer attached to the date. Do not rely on reposted flyers alone.

Confirm public access

Look for an official ticket or registration page. If none exists, ask whether spectators are admitted and whether advance registration is required.

Recheck before departure

Confirm the final timetable, gate, parking, weather notice and any schedule change on the organizer’s official channel.

Before leaving Pattaya

Build a circuit-day checklist

  • Official event name and organizer
  • Confirmed public, participant or accredited-media access
  • Ticket, registration or invitation saved offline
  • Final gate-opening and track timetable
  • Correct entrance and parking instructions
  • Return transport agreed in advance
  • Weather protection, hearing protection and water
  • Closed footwear and any paddock clothing requirement
  • Photography, drone and commercial-use rules
  • Circuit and organizer contact details

Official circuit contact

Confirm directly when the listing is unclear

Bira Circuit lists telephone 085-110-1000 and email info@bira.co.th. Ask a precise question with the event name and date.

Ask the right question

Copy this confirmation checklist

“I’m planning to visit [event name] at Bira Circuit on [date]. Is the event open to spectators? Do I need a ticket or advance registration? Which gate and parking area should I use, and what time does public entry begin?”

Frequently asked questions

Planning a Bira Circuit visit

Where is Bira Circuit?

Bira Circuit lists its official address as 111 Moo 5, Pong, Banglamung, Chonburi 20150. It is outside Pattaya’s walkable beach and nightlife areas, so plan road transport.

Can spectators visit Bira Circuit?

It depends on the specific event. A date may represent a private booking, test or organizer-controlled event. Confirm public admission, tickets, gate time and viewing areas before traveling.

Where can I find the event calendar?

Start with Bira Circuit’s official Track Calendar, then confirm a race through the championship or event organizer. RAAT also publishes an official annual competition calendar.

Can I drive my car or motorcycle on the track?

Only through an appropriate confirmed session, organizer or private booking. Ask about eligibility, license, vehicle preparation, helmet, safety inspection, briefing, insurance and fees.

Does a track reservation mean spectators can attend?

No. A reservation shows that the facility is booked; it does not establish public admission. The event owner must confirm access.

Should I buy tickets from a reseller?

Use the event organizer’s linked ticket source. If Bira’s calendar or the organizer does not identify the seller, verify it before paying.
