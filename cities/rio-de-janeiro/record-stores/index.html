Two record shops, one Botafogo outing

If you’re arriving by metro and browsing without a list, start at Baratos da Ribeiro near Botafogo station. If you’re looking for a particular Brazilian artist or pressing, contact Tropicália first and build the outing around whichever shop confirms it has a copy. Check both shops’ opening times before setting out.

Start with an artist or a label

Tropicália Discos

The shop’s own channel documents lesser-known Brazilian recordings. Ask for an LP that follows a record you already enjoy: the same arranger, a side project by the singer, or another release on the label. Say if you want vinyl, since the shop also describes selling CDs.

Rua Sorocaba 122 · Botafogo

Browse used samba and bossa nova

Baratos da Ribeiro

This used bookstore also buys, sells and exchanges LPs. Its vinyl catalogue lists samba, bossa nova and tropicalismo among its examples. Come with a style in mind, or bring a title and ask what’s on the shelf today.

Rua 19 de Fevereiro 90 · near Botafogo metro

How to buy the copy you actually want

  1. Bring a reference. Save a photo of the cover and note the label or catalogue number if the edition matters. Ask whether it’s a Brazilian pressing and whether it’s a reissue; those are different questions.
  2. Check the record and the sleeve separately. Ask whether you can inspect the disc or hear a short passage. A clean-looking sleeve doesn’t tell you how the quiet tracks play.
  3. Ask before crossing town. If you’re after one title, confirm the exact edition, condition and price with the shop, and ask whether they’ll hold it for pickup.
