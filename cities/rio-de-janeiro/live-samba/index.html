Three places to start, depending on what you want to hear

A roda at a social club

Samba do Trabalhador · Renascença Clube

Choose this for a specific roda rather than a general night out. Riotur identifies Samba do Trabalhador at Renascença Clube, and the club’s own 2026 archive documents repeated editions. Its address is Rua Barão de São Francisco, 54, in Andaraí.

Verify: Search the full roda name and confirm a dated edition with the club or producer. The photo archive shows past editions; it isn’t a calendar or proof of entry.

Confirm with Renascença Clube · Read Riotur’s roda overview

A bar built around samba

Beco do Rato · Lapa

The house describes itself as a samba bar with snacks and drinks at Rua Joaquim Silva, 11. Pick it when you want a Lapa stop with a named act, not when you need a guaranteed seated dinner.

Verify: Find the act in the agenda and ask whether admission, a table, and food are handled separately. If the agenda is a poster, read its date and year before following the booking instructions.

Check the venue agenda · Confirm the address with the venue

An event at a samba school

Acadêmicos do Salgueiro · Andaraí

Look to the school’s own announcements for the sound and setting of its quadra, the school’s event and rehearsal space. Salgueiro lists its quadra at Rua Silva Teles, 104. Past listings illustrate the difference between a community rehearsal with the bateria percussion section and a feijoada with a billed music program.

Verify: Confirm the current event is public, that it names the quadra and not another location, and what the ticket covers. A quadra event is not a Carnaval parade ticket.

Check Salgueiro’s official announcements

Read the Portuguese bill before buying

Roda de samba
A samba circle. Confirm the group, the place and whether this edition has a cover or ticket.
Ensaio de comunidade
A community rehearsal. Confirm public access and whether the percussion section is part of that date’s program.
Feijoada
A meal-centered event. Confirm when music begins and whether food is included or sold separately.
Ingresso · mesa
Ticket · table. If sitting matters, ask what kind of seat or table you are actually reserving. Check accessibility and agree on a return pickup before you leave.

In a roda, leave room for the circle

Follow the host’s lead. Keep paths clear, listen before joining in, and ask before filming people close up. If you want the outdoor Pedra do Sal option instead, start with the Rio nightlife hub.
