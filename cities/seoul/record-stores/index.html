Gimbab Records: start with genre

Gimbab Records’ current catalog separates vinyl across Korean music, pop and rock, electronic and dance, R&B, soul and funk, hip-hop, jazz and blues, reggae, soundtracks and more. Its own footer places the store on the third floor in Donggyo-dong, Mapo.

  • Write artist, title, label and acceptable format before browsing a large catalog.
  • Use the official address rather than a stale map listing.
  • Contact the shop when one pressing is the entire reason for the trip.

02

Clique Records: keep the stop multi-format

Clique Records’ first-party shop gives separate paths to vinyl, tapes and CDs, merchandise, books and gift cards. The retailer publishes a third-floor address on Euljiro 12-gil in Jung-gu, making it a focused Euljiro stop rather than a generic “central Seoul” listing.

  • Choose the format before opening the current catalog.
  • Confirm the physical shop details and item availability directly.
  • Pair it only with nearby Euljiro plans that preserve browsing time.

Check Clique Records directly

03

Welcome Records: include the playback question

Welcome Records separates music, equipment and vinyl-care sections on its current first-party site, and publishes a fifth-floor address off Apgujeong-ro in Gangnam. Choose it when the mission includes how records are played or maintained—not only which title to buy.

  • List the equipment or care question before visiting.
  • Ask what can actually be demonstrated; the site does not guarantee every item is on display.
  • Treat this as a separate Gangnam route, not a quick add-on to Mapo.

Build the dig

Search with a system, not a sprint

  1. Choose the mission. Broad genre discovery, multiple formats and playback care are different searches.
  2. Write the want list. Artist, title, label, format and acceptable condition make questions more precise.
  3. Verify the exact shop. Use the current first-party address and contact route before traveling.
  4. Check the physical item. Confirm pressing, condition, included inserts and purchase terms before paying.
  5. Protect the journey home. Keep records upright and away from heat, pressure and wet weather.

Useful restraint

One district can be enough

Mapo, Euljiro and Gangnam create different days. Give one store and its surrounding district enough time instead of turning the search into a transport race.

Common questions

District, stock and format

Which Seoul record store should I choose?

Choose Gimbab Records for a broad genre catalog, Clique Records for vinyl plus tapes, CDs, books and merchandise in Euljiro, or Welcome Records for music alongside equipment and vinyl-care categories in Gangnam.

Where are these stores?

Gimbab Records publishes a Donggyo-dong address in Mapo, Clique Records an Euljiro address in Jung-gu, and Welcome Records an Apgujeong-ro address in Gangnam. They work better as separate district plans than a quick crawl.

Can I rely on online stock?

No. A current catalog shows what a retailer offers, not that one title, pressing, format or accessory is on the physical shelf. Contact the shop before traveling for a specific item.
