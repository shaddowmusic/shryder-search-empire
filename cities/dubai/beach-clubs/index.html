  • Read the current age policy for every guest before booking.

Access before aesthetics

Compare the booking, not only the photograph

  1. Choose the day format. Quiet service, family time, pool-party energy and a social evening solve different plans.
  2. Use one date and group. Weekday, weekend, public holiday, adult count and child ages can change the terms.
  3. Read what the payment covers. Entry, sunbed, pool access, credit, food, drinks, cabana and cancellation are not universal.
  4. Check weather and operations. Outdoor access, events and seating can change; verify with the venue before leaving.
  5. Map the return. Use Dubai’s official RTA planner for the actual date and time, then set a legal fallback where public transport is impractical.

Beach-club questions

Choice, passes and guest rules

Which Dubai beach club should I choose?

Choose DRIFT for adults-oriented calm and polished service, Riva for a family-friendly Palm day, BCH:CLB for music-led pool energy, or Surf Club for a social Palm West Beach route that can continue into the evening.

Do Dubai beach clubs sell day passes?

Access models vary by venue and date. Some publish pool-and-beach access, sunbeds or cabanas; others organize the visit around a reservation. Compare the current minimum spend, included credit, age policy and cancellation conditions before paying.

Are children allowed?

Policies differ. Riva currently describes itself as family friendly, while DRIFT describes an adults-only concept and publishes specific age conditions. Check the exact venue policy for the visit date rather than assuming one citywide rule.
