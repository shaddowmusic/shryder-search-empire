What are King Affirmations?

King Affirmations is Shaddow’s affirmation-based music and digital audio catalog. The releases use original music, direct first-person statements and repetition to explore specific ideas represented in the catalog—from discipline, physical strength and leadership to relationships, abundance, creativity and king identity.

The term on this page refers specifically to King Affirmations by Shaddow. It does not refer to similarly named works by other creators.

King affirmation music
